Privacy Policy

GIMT Team · Last updated 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

GIMT Team ("we", "us") runs the team dashboard at gimtteam.vercel.app and the Telegram bot that supports it. This page explains what we collect when you use them, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

2. What we collect

Data When Why
Telegram user ID, first name and username You open the dashboard from the Telegram bot To confirm who you are and show your own work
Google account email address You choose "Continue with Google" To check that you are on the team roster before signing you in
Work records you create: proof-of-work submissions, page documents, uploaded images, activity log entries You submit or edit them in the bot or dashboard To run the team's work tracking
Group message statistics (counts per member and per group) The bot records activity in the team's Telegram group To report team activity

We do not ask for passwords, payment details, contacts, location, or any other Google account data.

3. Cookies

We set two cookies, both essential to signing in. Neither is used for advertising or tracking.

Cookie Purpose Lifetime
gimt_session Keeps you signed in. It holds your member ID and name, signed so it cannot be forged. 12 hours
gimt_oauth Carries the one-time security code that completes a Google sign-in. 10 minutes

Both cookies are HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read them. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

4. Google user data

When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name and profile picture URL from Google. We store only the email address, and only to match you to a member on the team roster. If your email is not on the roster, no account is created and no session is issued.

GIMT Team's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties except to the service providers listed below that operate the app on our behalf.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — database, authentication and image storage.
  • Vercel — hosting for the dashboard and its API.
  • Telegram — the bot and Mini App that open the dashboard.
  • Google — optional sign-in with a Google account.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

Work records and activity logs stay while the team uses them. Session cookies expire on their own (12 hours and 10 minutes). If you ask us to delete your data, we remove your roster entry, your sign-in records and the work records you created, unless we must keep something for a legal or accounting reason.

7. Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Sign out at any time with the Disconnect button, which clears the session cookie.
  • Revoke our access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Security

Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies. Google sign-in runs on our server: the one-time code is exchanged server-side and no Google token is ever sent to your browser. Access to the dashboard is limited to members on the team roster.

9. Children

The dashboard is a workplace tool and is not intended for anyone under 13.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced in the team's Telegram group.
